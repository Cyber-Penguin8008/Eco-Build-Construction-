Our story

Eco Construction-Build was founded in 2022 by Gayrat Ergashev, a builder who believes every job deserves to be done properly. Since then he has brought together a team of trusted tradespeople, from plumbers and plasterers to tilers, carpenters, roofers and decorators.

Gayrat manages each project personally and brings in the right specialists at the right time. The result is work that runs smoothly, finishes cleanly and lasts.
