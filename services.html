Services

Everything your home needs, from fresh paint to a new roof, managed by one trusted team.

Painting

Interior and exterior painting and decorating, with careful preparation for a clean, lasting finish.

Plumbing

New pipework, taps, sinks, toilets and showers fitted, and leaks found and repaired.

Plasterboard

Plasterboard walls, partitions and ceilings, put up straight, solid and ready to finish.

Tiling

Wall and floor tiling for kitchens, bathrooms and hallways, laid neatly and sealed to last.

Flooring

Carpet and parquet flooring, fitted with care for a smooth, even finish.

Bathrooms

Complete bathroom fitting, from plumbing and tiling to the final finishing touches.

Kitchens
